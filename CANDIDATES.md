# Kandidat Cerita Autocorrect Fail

Kriteria penilaian: harus jelas soal autocorrect, punchline di akhir, ada detail kata asal vs kata ganti plus akibatnya, terdengar baru, dan langsung nyambung dalam sekali baca.

1. I meant to text my boss "I need a raise" but it sent "I need a raisin" and now HR wants to discuss my snack demands.
2. Tried to write "ducking exhausted" after my shift and my phone changed it to the honest version.
3. I typed "see you at the funeral" and it became "funereal" — even my keyboard thought I was being too cheerful.
4. Texted my landlord "the sink is leaking" — it sent "the sink is freaking" and he replied "same."
5. I wrote "I'm at the dentist" and it sent "I'm at the dentists' cult" — my mom called the police.
6. Meant to say "good luck on your exam" — it sent "good lick on your exam" and my friend blocked me.
7. I typed "please pass the salt" at dinner and it autocorrected to "please pass the assault" — grandma left the table.
8. Tried to text "I'm running late" and it sent "I'm ruining late" — my date said "at least you're honest."
9. I wrote "happy birthday, hope you get cake" and it sent "hope you get caked" — my coworker filed a complaint.
10. Texted my trainer "my legs are sore" — it became "my legs are sorry" and he said "they should be."
11. I meant "call me when you're free" and it sent "call me when you're flee" — now my brother thinks I'm in trouble.
12. Typed "I love you, mom" and it sent "I love you, mop" — she replied with a photo of cleaning supplies.

## Pemilihan Pairwise

Ronde awal, saya buang yang punchline-nya lemah atau akibatnya tidak jelas: #4 lucu tapi tidak ada kata ganti yang menonjol, #8 terlalu mirip format lelucon lama, #11 agak dipaksakan ejaannya.

Yang bertahan sampai akhir: #1, #2, #3, #6, #7, #10, #12. Dari situ #2 menang karena singkat, sangat on-topic (autocorrect ducking adalah klasik), dan punchline "honest version" di akhir membalik ekspektasi — pembaca langsung paham tanpa penjelasan. #1 kuat tapi panjang dan HR snack demands terasa dibuat-buat. #7 bagus tapi assault/salt sudah terlalu sering dipakai. #2 paling segar, bersih, dan aman sekali baca.

Pemenang: #2, dengan sedikit penajaman kata agar ritmenya pas.
